    Controls and shortcuts
    • Drag a piece to move it. It slides along whatever blocks it and never overlaps.
    • Mouse snap: drag near a polygon corner or edge for magnetic contact. Matching faces align when their angles are within 6°. A guide marks the contact; hold Option / Alt for free movement, or choose Corners, Edges or Off in the toolbar. Selected groups snap without changing their relative positions.
    • Rotate: drag the ⟳ handle, scroll the wheel over a selected piece, twist with two fingers, or press Q / E (1°), Shift+Q / E (15°), Alt+Q / E (0.1°).
    • Arrow keys move by 0.1; Alt+arrow by 0.01; Shift+arrow snaps to contact.
    • Multi-select: Shift-click pieces, Shift-drag a box on empty space, Ctrl+A, or the Multi-select button on touch screens.
    • Touch: drag a piece with one finger; with a piece selected, twist two fingers to rotate it; with nothing selected, pinch to zoom.
    • View: drag empty space to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, F to fit, + / − to zoom.
    • Container: drag the φ handle on its corner, type φ, or use Auto-orient.
    • Keyboard only: focus the board, A adds a piece, N / P select the next / previous piece (Shift+N / P extend the selection), Esc deselects, Delete removes the selection, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo and redo.
