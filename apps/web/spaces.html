Spaces

The same unit pieces, packed in other spaces: on the surface of a Platonic solid, in a container at constant curvature, on the whole sphere and on flat tori. Each space has its own problems, a viewer that draws its records, and notes that define it exactly. The board opens every problem too: choose the space in its Problem section, or follow a row's Open on the board link, to run the optimizer in your browser and submit a better packing.

Each problem shows two values side by side. The certified value is PolyPack's research record: its packing is proven valid with interval arithmetic. A site entry is a packing sent to this site; the server checks it with floating-point arithmetic and a tolerance of 10−9, like every other entry. Entries go through the API. The volunteer client can also work on these problems when its optional spaces mode is turned on.

      Records

      Space

      Certified value
      The research record of the problem. Its packing is proven valid with interval arithmetic at this size. Shown at 6 decimals.
      Status
      Best known; proven optimal, where a published proof covers the problem; or no packing exists, where it is proved that the pieces do not fit. Proven and impossible problems take no entries.
      Lower bound
      No packing of the problem can be smaller. Shown at 6 decimals, with what it rests on.
      Site best
      The best entry sent to this site, checked by the server's floating-point validator of the space. Only the leading entry of each problem is kept. Shown at 6 decimals.
      Configuration
      The problem with its packings as JSON, in the space's own format (see its notes).
      Board
      Opens the problem on the board: its best packing drawn, the optimizer in your browser, and submission of a better packing.
