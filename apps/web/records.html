Without folding, each face holds its pieces on its own. So a no-fold problem with P pieces on a solid with F faces is the same as packing ⌈P / F⌉ pieces in one face, a 2D in 2D problem. Each no-fold row links to it.

Record
The smaller of the research record and the site's leading entry, at 6 decimals. A research record's packing is proven valid with interval arithmetic, or for 3D on 4D with exact rational arithmetic.
Status
Best known; proven optimal, where a published proof covers the problem; or no packing exists, where it is proved that the pieces do not fit.
Site entry, no research record
The problem has no research record yet, and a packing sent to this site leads it. Its lower bound, where known, is from the kind's notes.
Held by
A site entry is a packing sent to this site and checked by the server. Otherwise the record is PolyPack's research record.
Lower bound
No packing of the problem can be smaller. The gap is the record minus the bound.
