Record holders

Who leads the problems on this site, in every Dimension, counted by the exact name submitted with each entry: names that differ in any character, capitals included, are counted separately. Problems with a proven optimum take no entries and are left out. Select the heading of a column to sort the table by it, and again to turn the order round.

Total records held
Problems in any Dimension where the name has the best entry on the site, and that entry is at least as good as the published value, to the precision the source prints, or there is no published value to compare with. A claim that beats a published value counts once it has been checked. 3D on 4D and the higher Dimensions have no column of their own; their records count here.
